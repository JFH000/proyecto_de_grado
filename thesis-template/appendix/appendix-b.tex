\chapter{Detailed results}
\label{apx:detailed-results}

This appendix gathers the final values of all the measures, with the agents learning from the individual
reward (Table~\ref{tab:apx-individual}) and from the shared reward (Table~\ref{tab:apx-shared}); the variability
across seeds of the measures of quality of service and behavior (Table~\ref{tab:apx-variability}); and the
computation time of each run (Table~\ref{tab:apx-time}). As in Chapter~\ref{chap:results}, the final value of a
measure is its mean over the last 200 episodes, and the measures are those of Section~\ref{sec:exp-metrics}.
Greedy and the threshold rule do not learn, so they give the same results in both sweeps.

The columns of the first two tables are: the shared reward per episode (mean $\pm$ standard deviation across
seeds); the mean rate of the \acsp{CUE}, in Mbps; the percentage of slots in outage, which includes the slots in
which the pair stays silent; the mean delay, in milliseconds; the number of different channels used by the pairs
that transmit; the mean power, as a fraction of the maximum; and the percentage of slots in which a pair
transmits on a channel on which another pair also transmits.

\section{Computation time}

Table~\ref{tab:apx-time} gives the mean time of a run of 2000 episodes in the sweeps of the individual and the
shared reward, with two processor cores per run. The runs were spread over 12 nodes of the cluster, so part of
the differences between methods comes from the node on which each one ran. The fixed rules take minutes,
because they do not train any network; the learning methods take hours, and their time grows with $K$, since
every agent trains its own network in every slot.
